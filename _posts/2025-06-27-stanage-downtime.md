From 30th June 2025, our High Performance Computing (HPC) platform, Stanage, will be unavailable for several days so that various important activities can be performed during a maintenance window.


Period of downtime for Stanage: **From 08:00 on 30th June 2025 until 17:00 on 4th July 2025**


We will try to minimise this downtime and it is possible that Stanage will return to service before 4th July. We will notify you by email when Stanage is back online and available for job submission.


Reason for this work:

* Apply firmware and driver updates
* Improve aspects of system resilience and power management
* Expand the home directory area as it is reaching capacity
* Upgrade the Slurm job scheduler


This work is necessary and we regret any disruption caused.


If you have any questions or concerns please get in touch via [research-it@sheffield.ac.uk](mailto:research-it@sheffield.ac.uk?subject=RE:Stanage downtime for maintenance: 30th June - 4th July 2025).
