This is an early notification that Bessemer will cease service at end of day on **Friday 31st October 2025**.

**Action Required:**

Transfer all needed data from ``/home`` and ``/fastdata`` to Stanage.

Check if your required software is available on Stanage — raise a ticket if not.

Test workloads on Stanage.

Request ``/shared`` (Research Storage) access on Stanage via research-it@sheffield.ac.uk, confirming no sensitive data is stored there.

**Note:** ``/shared`` is not accessible on Stanage compute nodes — you must stage data via login nodes.

Onboarding sessions coming soon. Start planning your transition now.
